Questions
What is your topic?
We would like to examine housing prices across the United States and explore how various factors have affected those prices across time. One of the ideas that we have is to examine how the growth of colleges in smaller towns (transforming those towns into “college towns”) affects the housing prices of those specific areas. We hope to examine how the presence and the quantity of on-campus housing for colleges affects these changes in price as well. 
Why is it socially or scientifically significant?
Housing is a basic human need. Housing prices are reflective of a large number of factors and are often examined as one of the key aspects of “affordability” within the country, state, and county level. By examining even how some factors tend to influence housing prices, we hope to shed a light on the kinds of things that—perhaps unexpectedly—have an effect on affordability and cost-of-living in general.
Who is your audience? Why are they reading your article? What are your assumptions about them?
Our audience pertains to (incoming) college students looking into housing or schools, renters, and home-buyers considering options of where to live. We assume that they understand that rent pertains to monthly cost and that they are aware that rent can drastically change from state to state. We will also assume that they are able to link the density of a county to an increase in demand and therefore an increase in housing pricing.
